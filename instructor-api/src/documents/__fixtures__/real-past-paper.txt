SECTION D: WORKED SOLUTIONS (Questions 61 – 70 Highlights)
Q62 Solution: $\text{Throughput} = N \times R \times T = 2 \text{ cranes} \times 12 \text{ cont/hr} \times 10 \text{ hrs} = \mathbf{240\text{
containers/day}}$.
Q63 Solution:
(a) $\text{Payload} = W_{gvw} - W_{tare} = 24,000\text{ kg} - 8,500\text{ kg} = \mathbf{15,500\text{ kg}}$.
(b) $\text{Pallets} = \lfloor 15,500 / 750 \rfloor = \lfloor 20.66 \rfloor = \mathbf{20\text{ pallets}}$.
Q67 Solution: (a) System capacity = minimum leg rate = $\mathbf{60\text{ tons/hour}}$. (b) Leg B is the bottleneck. Mitigation: Reroute
heavy loads over alternative routes, stage cargo, or employ temporary heavy bridging structures.
Q70 Solution:
$\text{Driving Time} = 300\text{ km} / 60\text{ km/h} = 5.0\text{ hours}$.
$\text{Delays} = (2 \times 30\text{ mins}) + 60\text{ mins} = 2.0\text{ hours}$.
$\text{Total Transit Time} = 5.0 + 2.0 = \mathbf{7.0\text{ hours}}$.
